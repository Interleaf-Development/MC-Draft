# MathConcept demo conventions

- Parent and Student interfaces use **Hong Kong Traditional Chinese (`zh-HK`)** by default, including new features, dialogs, validation, accessibility labels, sample learning content and PWA metadata. Use natural Hong Kong wording: 家長、學生、老師、課堂、補堂、工作紙、繳費、收據、付款證明.
- Parent layouts target phones; Student layouts target large tablets such as iPad Pro.
- Admin, Teacher, Parent and Student demo interfaces use Hong Kong Traditional Chinese (`zh-HK`), including the standalone demo and proposal embeds. The proposal text remains available in Chinese and English. Preserve canonical values and user-authored content when translating interfaces.
- Use the explicit family locale helpers or Chinese source strings in family-only screens. Preserve identifiers, action names, canonical model values, mathematical notation, names and uploaded filenames. Do not translate or overwrite user-authored messages, notes or worksheet answers.
- Keep the demo frontend-only. Local data does not sync across devices. OCR/payment checks remain explicitly simulated.
- Tsuen Wan (`/`) and Hang Hau (`/hh/`) share all application and workflow code. Future changes apply to both. Use `branch-config.js` for branch identity/teachers/paths; keep their saved demo data and family PWA identities separate. Preserve existing Tsuen Wan browser data.
- New locally imported modules and essential artwork must be added to the service worker cache. Bump its cache version when the asset set changes.
- Run `npm run check` and `npm test`; check changed family flows at phone/tablet widths before publishing. Vercel deploys `dist/` from `main`.
- The English and Hong Kong Chinese proposal in `dist/proposal/` is client-facing. Describe capabilities, business outcomes, workflows, scope and acceptance criteria. Keep stack choices, supplier/library names, architecture and implementation techniques out of the proposal. Retain accurate demo/proposed distinctions and practical limits. Present it as a compact product scope and delivery document, with descriptive headings, visible requirements and real interactive demos shown inline immediately after the relevant chapter introduction; avoid landing-page slogans or decorative cover artwork.
- Do not state or estimate the number of files in the client-facing proposal; refer to the existing teaching-material library.
- Proposal demos preload automatically and stay mounted across chapter changes. Do not gate them behind an Open button; keep shared demo records and student selection consistent when switching screens.

- Billing supports a centre choice: AI checks followed by staff approval (default), or automatic receipt issuance only when every required proof check passes. Missing, unclear, mismatched or duplicate evidence always needs staff review. The demo simulates AI checks and does not confirm actual bank settlement. Changing approval mode applies to future proof submissions, not existing queued records. Recurring tuition is HK$2,000 for a nominal eight-lesson package valid for two calendar months; students have individual periods (for example September–October or October–November). The usual seven, eight or nine lessons in a period do not change the fee or create a refund; teaching schedules average 48 weeks annually. Invoice on the 20th of the month before the next period, due on the following month’s 20th (October–November: issue 20 September, due 20 October). A regular schedule change from x to x+1 lessons requires an admin choice to allow the extra lesson at the same fee or exclude it; x to x−1 requires a choice whether to grant make-up credit. Approved amended receipts list actual lessons, retain the original payment and receipt dates and paid amount, and record the actual amendment date and reason while preserving all previous versions. Only first-enrolment payment approval activates enrolment. Preserve historical receipts when updating demo fixtures.
